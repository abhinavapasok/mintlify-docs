**Create sales quickly, accept payments, and generate invoices from the point of sale.**

<Note>
  The POS module is designed for quick sales transactions. You can select products, add them to the cart, choose a customer, review pricing and taxes, accept payment, and complete the sale.
</Note>

## Overview

The POS module allows you to create and complete sales transactions from a single screen.

You can use POS to:

- Search for products by name or SKU
- Browse products by category
- Add products to the cart
- Select a customer
- Increase or decrease item quantities
- Remove items from the cart
- Review subtotal, tax, and total
- Modify price details
- Add notes
- Add terms and conditions
- Hold a bill for later
- Select a payment method
- Select a payment account
- Enter the amount received
- Complete the payment
- Print the receipt
- Send the invoice
- Start the next sale

## Before you start

- **Permission needed:** Access to the **POS** module.
- **Settings to review:** Make sure your products, customers, taxes, payment methods, and payment accounts are configured before processing sales.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| POS | Point of Sale used to create and complete sales transactions. |
| Cart | Contains the products added to the current sale. |
| Customer | The customer associated with the sale. |
| Payment method | The method used to receive payment, such as cash or card. |
| Payment account | The account into which the payment is recorded. |
| Balance due | The amount remaining to be paid after entering the amount received. |
| Hold Bill | Temporarily saves the current sale so it can be completed later. |
| Invoice | The sales document generated after completing the transaction. |

# Common workflows

## Create a POS sale

1. Open **POS** from the navigation menu.
2. Search for a product using **Search by name or SKU**.
3. Select the required product to add it to the cart.
4. Select a **Customer**, if applicable.
5. Review the products and quantities in the cart.
6. Use the **\+** and **−** buttons to adjust quantities.
7. Remove an item using the delete option, if required.
8. Review the subtotal, tax, and total.
9. Use **Modify Price Details** if price details need to be changed.
10. Add a note or terms and conditions, if required.
11. Click **Charge** to proceed to payment.

## Select a customer

The customer section is available on the POS screen.

1. Click the **Customer** field.
2. Search for the required customer.
3. Select the customer.
4. Review the customer details and outstanding balance displayed in the customer section.

## Add products to the cart

Products can be added directly from the product list.

1. Search for the product by **name or SKU**, or browse the available products.
2. Select the required product.
3. The product is added to the cart.
4. Review the quantity and price.
5. Add additional products as required.

You can also use the category options to filter the displayed products.

## Update item quantity

1. Locate the item in the cart.
2. Click **\+** to increase the quantity.
3. Click **−** to decrease the quantity.
4. Review the updated cart total.

## Remove an item

1. Locate the item in the cart.
2. Click the **Delete** icon.
3. The item is removed from the current cart.

## Review price details

Use **Modify Price Details** to review or modify the applicable price information before completing the sale.

After making changes, review the updated subtotal, tax, and total before proceeding to payment.

## Add notes and terms

You can add additional information to the sale.

- Click **\+ Add Note** to enter a note.
- Click **\+ Terms & Conditions** to add applicable terms and conditions.

## Hold a bill

Use **Hold Bill** when you need to temporarily keep the current sale without completing the payment.

1. Add the required products to the cart.
2. Review the sale.
3. Click **Hold Bill**.
4. Continue with the sale later according to the POS workflow.

# Accept a payment

After adding the products and reviewing the sale:

1. Click **Charge**.
2. Select the required payment method.
3. Select the **Payment account**.
4. Enter the **Amount Received**.
5. Review the **Balance Due**.
6. Click **Charge** to complete the payment.

## Available payment methods

The payment screen provides the following payment methods:

- **Cash**
- **Credit Card**
- **Debit Card**
- **Bank**
- **Cheque**

<Note>
  The available payment methods depend on the payment options configured in KomKits.
</Note>

## Payment account

The **Payment account** identifies the account where the payment is recorded.

1. Select the required payment method.
2. Select the appropriate payment account.
3. Enter the amount received.
4. Review the balance due.
5. Complete the payment.

# Payment successful

After a successful payment, the payment confirmation screen displays:

- Payment status
- Payment mode
- Invoice number
- Customer name
- Total amount
- Paid amount
- Balance

You can then choose one of the available actions:

### Print Receipt

Click **Print Receipt** to print the receipt for the completed sale.

### Send Invoice

Click **Send Invoice** to send the generated invoice to the customer.

### Next Sale

Click **Next Sale** to return to the POS screen and start a new transaction.

# FAQs

### What is POS?

POS (Point of Sale) is used to create sales transactions, collect payments, and generate invoices quickly.

### Can I search for a product?

Yes. Products can be searched using their **name or SKU**.

### Can I change the quantity of an item?

Yes. Use the **\+** and **−** controls in the cart to update the quantity.

### Can I remove an item from the cart?

Yes. Click the delete icon next to the item you want to remove.

### What payment methods are available?

The POS payment screen currently provides **Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank, and Cheque** options.

### Can I add a customer to a POS sale?

Yes. You can select a customer from the **Customer** section.

### Can I hold a sale without completing payment?

Yes. Use **Hold Bill** to temporarily hold the current transaction.

### Can I print a receipt after payment?

Yes. After a successful payment, click **Print Receipt**.

### Can I send the invoice after completing a POS sale?

Yes. After successful payment, use **Send Invoice** from the payment confirmation screen.

### What happens after a successful payment?

The payment confirmation screen displays the transaction details and provides options to **Print Receipt**, **Send Invoice**, or proceed to **Next Sale**.
