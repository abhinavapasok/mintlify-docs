**Manage the people and businesses you trade with.**

## Overview

Customers and suppliers are the parties your business interacts with for sales and purchases. In KomKits, you can create and manage their contact information, addresses, balances, and transaction history from a single place.

You can use this section to:

- Create and manage customers and suppliers
- Store contact and business information
- Add billing and shipping addresses
- View outstanding balances
- View sales and purchase transactions
- Edit or deactivate party records
- Track payment and transaction history

## Before you start

- **Permission needed:** Access to the **Customers/Suppliers** or **Party** module.
- **Settings to review:** Business, tax, currency, and payment settings should be configured before creating transactions.

## Key concepts

### Customers

Customers are individuals or businesses who purchase products or services from your business.

A customer record can contain:

- Customer/business name
- Contact person
- Phone number
- Email address
- Billing address
- Shipping address
- Tax/GST information
- Opening balance
- Payment terms

### Suppliers

Suppliers are individuals or businesses from whom your business purchases products or services.

Supplier records can contain:

- Supplier/business name
- Contact person
- Phone number
- Email address
- Billing address
- Shipping address
- Tax/GST information
- Opening balance
- Payment terms

## Create a customer or supplier

1. Open **Customers and Suppliers** from the main navigation.
2. Select **Add Customer** or **Add Supplier**.
3. Enter the required details.
4. Add contact and address information.
5. Enter tax and payment details if applicable.
6. Click **Save**.

## View party details

Open a customer or supplier record to view:

- Contact information
- Addresses
- Outstanding balance
- Sales or purchase transactions
- Payment history
- Related invoices and documents

## Edit a customer or supplier

1. Open the customer or supplier record.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Update the required information.
4. Click **Save**.

## Deactivate a customer or supplier

If a party is no longer active, you can deactivate the record instead of deleting it.

Deactivated parties should no longer appear as active options when creating new transactions, while their historical transactions remain available.
