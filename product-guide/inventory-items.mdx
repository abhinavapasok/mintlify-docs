## Overview

The Inventory and items module helps you manage the products and services used in your business. You can create and maintain item records, organize items using categories and other details, manage pricing, and track stock levels.

Use this module to keep your item information up to date and ensure that the correct items, prices, and stock quantities are available when creating sales and purchase transactions.

## Before you start

- **Permission needed:** Access to the **Inventory and items** module.
- **Settings to review:** Review your business, tax, and inventory settings before creating items.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Item | A product or service that can be used in business transactions. |
| Stock | The quantity of an item currently available in inventory. |
| Category | A way to organize related items. |
| Brand | Identifies the brand associated with an item, when applicable. |
| Manufacturer | Identifies the manufacturer associated with an item, when applicable. |
| Stock adjustment | A change made to an item's available stock quantity. |

## Common workflows 

## Add an item

Create an item to make a product or service available for use in transactions.

1. Open **items**.
2. Click **Add item**.
3. Enter the required item details.
4. Enter pricing and inventory information, if applicable.
5. Review the information.
6. Click **Save**.

### Edit an item

1. Open **items**.
2. Select the item you want to update.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Update the required information.
5. Click **Save**.

### Adjust stock

Use stock adjustment when the available quantity needs to be corrected or updated.

1. Open  **items**.
2. Select the item.
3. Open the adjustment stock option.
4. Enter the required quantity or adjustment details.
5. Review the updated stock quantity.
6. Save the adjustment.

### Field reference

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Item name | Identifies the product or service. |
| Category | Groups the item with related items. |
| Brand | Identifies the item's brand, when applicable. |
| Manufacturer | Identifies the item's manufacturer, when applicable. |
| Price | Defines the applicable selling or item price. |
| Stock | Shows the available quantity for a stock-tracked item. |

## FAQs

### Can I create both products and services as items?

Yes, depending on the item type supported by your KomKits configuration.

### Can I update an item after creating it?

Yes. Open the item and use the **Edit** option to update its available details.

### Can I adjust an item's stock?

Yes. Stock can be updated using the stock adjustment functionality.
