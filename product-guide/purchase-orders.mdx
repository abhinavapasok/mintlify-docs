<Note>
  Purchase orders help you record items you intend to purchase from suppliers, including quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, payment terms, and delivery details.
</Note>

## Overview

The Purchase orders module allows you to create purchase orders for suppliers before completing a purchase.

You can use this module to:

- Create purchase orders
- Select a supplier
- Set the order date and due date
- Define payment terms
- Add products to the purchase order
- Specify item quantities and prices
- Apply discounts and tax categories
- Add notes
- Add terms and conditions
- Review the grand total
- Create and manage purchase orders

## Before you start

- **Permission needed:** Access to the **Purchase orders** module.
- **Settings to review:** Make sure suppliers, items, tax settings, and payment-related settings are configured before creating a purchase order.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase order | A document used to request or authorize the purchase of goods or services from a supplier. |
| Supplier | The party from whom the goods or services are being purchased. |
| Order date | The date on which the purchase order is created. |
| Due date | The date associated with the expected payment or due date. |
| Payment terms | Defines the agreed payment terms for the purchase. |
| Tax category | Specifies the tax category applicable to an item. |
| Grand total | The final calculated amount of the purchase order. |

## Common workflows

## Create a purchase order

1. Open **Purchase orders** from the navigation menu.
2. Click **New** or **Create Purchase Order**.
3. Enter the **Order Date**.
4. Enter the **Due Date**.
5. Select the **Payment Terms**.
6. Select a **Supplier** using **Search Supplier**.
7. Review the **Bill From Address** and **Ship From Address**.
8. Add the required items.
9. Enter the quantity for each item.
10. Review the item price.
11. Apply a discount or select a tax category, if applicable.
12. Add **Notes**, if required.
13. Add **Terms and Conditions**, if required.
14. Review the **Grand Total**.
15. Click **Create Purchase Order**.

## Field reference

| Field | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order Date | Yes | Specifies the purchase order date. |
| Due Date | Yes | Specifies the due date associated with the purchase order. |
| Payment Terms | No\* | Defines the payment terms for the purchase. |
| Purchase Order Number | System-generated | Displays the purchase order number assigned by the system. |
| Supplier | Yes | Selects the supplier associated with the purchase order. |
| Bill From Address | — | Displays the supplier's billing address. |
| Ship From Address | — | Displays the supplier's shipping address. |
| Filter by Category | No | Filters the available items by category. |
| Search for existing products | No | Searches for products to add to the purchase order. |
| Quantity | Yes for added items | Specifies the quantity of the selected item. |
| Notes | No | Adds additional information to the purchase order. |
| Terms and Conditions | No | Adds terms and conditions to the purchase order. |
| Grand Total | System-calculated | Displays the total amount of the purchase order. |

## FAQs

### What is a purchase order?

A purchase order is a document used to record goods or services that you intend to purchase from a supplier.

### Can I add multiple items to a purchase order?

Yes. You can search for products and add multiple items to the **Items** section.

### Can I apply a discount to an item?

Yes. The item table includes a **Discount** field.

### Can I apply tax to an item?

Yes. You can select the applicable **Tax Category** for an item.

### Can I add notes to a purchase order?

Yes. You can add additional information in the **Notes** field.

### Can I add terms and conditions?

Yes. You can enter applicable terms in the **Terms and Conditions** field.

### Is the Purchase Order Number entered manually?

No. The screenshot shows that the **Purchase Order Number** is automatically assigned when the purchase order is saved.
