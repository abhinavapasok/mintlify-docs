## Overview

The Payments module helps you record payments received from customers and payments made to suppliers.

You can use the module to:

- Record money received from a party
- Record money paid to a party
- Select the payment mode
- Select the account where the payment is received or paid
- Enter the payment amount
- Add notes for additional payment details
- Track payment records

## Before you start

- **Permission needed:** Access to the **Payments** module.
- **Settings to review:** Make sure the required payment accounts and payment modes are configured before recording a payment.

Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Payment in | Money received from a customer or other party. |
| Payment out | Money paid to a supplier or other party. |
| Party | The customer, supplier, or other business party associated with the payment. |
| Payment mode | The method used to make or receive the payment, such as cash. |
| Payment account | The account through which the payment is received or paid. |

## Common workflows

### Record a payment in

Use **Payment in** to record money received from a party.

1. Open **Payments** from the navigation menu.
2. Select **Payment in**.
3. Select the **Party**.
4. Select the **Payment date**.
5. Select the **Payment mode**.
6. Select the account in **Payment Received In**.
7. Enter the **Payment amount**.
8. Add **Notes** if required.
9. Click **Create Payment**.

### Record a payment out

Use **Payment out** to record money paid to a party.

1. Open **Payments** from the navigation menu.
2. Select **Payment out**.
3. Select the required party.
4. Select the payment date.
5. Select the payment mode.
6. Select the payment account.
7. Enter the payment amount.
8. Add notes if required.
9. Click **Create Payment**.

## FAQs

### What is Payment in?

Payment in is used to record money received from a party.

### What is Payment out?

Payment out is used to record money paid to a party.

### Is Party required when recording a payment?

Yes. **Party** is a required field on the Payment in form.

### Is the payment date required?

Yes. **Payment date** is a required field.

### Can I add a new payment account while recording a payment?

The Payment in form provides a **\+** button beside the **Payment Received In** field for adding an account.
