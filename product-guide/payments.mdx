</Note>

## Overview

The Payments module helps you record payments received from customers and payments made to suppliers.

You can use the module to:

- Record money received from a party
- Record money paid to a party
- Select the payment mode
- Select the account where the payment is received or paid
- Enter the payment amount
- Add notes for additional payment details
- Track payment records

## Before you start

- **Permission needed:** Access to the **Payments** module.
- **Settings to review:** Make sure the required payment accounts and payment modes are configured before recording a payment.

Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Payment in | Money received from a customer or other party. |
| Payment out | Money paid to a supplier or other party. |
| Party | The customer, supplier, or other business party associated with the payment. |
| Payment mode | The method used to make or receive the payment, such as cash. |
| Payment account | The account through which the payment is received or paid. |
